Finding Your Gateway

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap on Wi-Fi.
  3. Tap the i (info) or gear icon next to the Wi-Fi network you are connected to.
  4. Scroll down to the IPv4 Address or Network Details section.
  5. The number listed next to Router or Gateway is your default gateway. (Something that looks like 192.168.0.1 or 10.0.0.1)
