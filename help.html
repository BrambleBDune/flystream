DNS Help

  1. Go to wifi settings and do a long press on your wifi name.
  2. Take note of your IP address. It should be something along the lines of 192.168.1.1 or 10.0.0.138.
  3. Find and note your routers Gateway IP address on your phone.
  4. Once you have all your IP's go back to you wifi on you ONN tv and long press. once in there go down to IP settings and click.
  5. Click Static and follow the guide.
  6. For Network Prefix Length put "24"
  7. For DNS 1 put "1.1.1.1"
  8. For DNS 2 put "8.8.8.8"
  9. After that you should be good to go!
  10. If you run into trouble or want to change it back then go back to IP settings and click DHCP
